# Short Ramified Columns & Archimedean Size Cancellation

This page explains the prime-by-prime divisibility ledger and short ramified spanning basis (`src/New/UpperBound/Size/ShortColumns.lean`), together with the Stirling–Chowla–Selberg archimedean size cancellation and good-projection bounds (`src/New/UpperBound/Size/ArchimedeanSize.lean`). Return to the [Stage 03 Overview](./overview.md), review the period identity that requires ramification at $\pi_N$ in [The Iterated Seesaw Identity](./seesaw-identity.md), or continue to [Local Divisibility & Elementary Divisors](./local-divisibility-and-elementary-divisors.md).

## Level 1 — First-Principles

Stage 03 applies a chain of $m$ lifts via the {{concept:theta-correspondence|machine that transports automorphic forms from one unitary group to a larger one through a pairing integral}}—moving from a small source space $X_0$ through intermediate spaces $X_1, \dots, X_m$ and finally restricting to a subspace $Y_m$. To feed Stage 01's {{concept:twisted-lattice|structure that measures ordinary lengths in one fixed standard space while checking divisibility across prime-dependent auxiliary spaces}}, Stage 03 must control two opposing quantities at once: the ordinary archimedean size that bounds the {{concept:gram-determinant|squared volume enclosed by a list of vectors, computed from their pairwise inner products}}, and the prime-by-prime divisibility that enters Stage 01's {{concept:arithmetic-height|global accounting rule balancing ordinary size against prime-by-prime divisibility}}.

A crucial structural split governs both files, mirroring Stage 01's {{term:twisted-lattice}} contract: **ordinary sizes (and hence {{term:gram-determinant|covolumes}}) are measured exclusively at the trivial twist ($\vartheta = 1$, with empty ramification set $Q = \emptyset$)**, while prime-by-prime {{term:arithmetic-height|p-adic valuations}} are read across prime-avoiding twists $\vartheta$. Four mechanisms carry this archimedean side of Stage 03:

1. **Fix a shared prime-by-prime ledger.** Before lifting any form, `ShortColumns.lean` defines the exact divisibility target $\mathrm{gain}(p)$ requested at every non-split {{concept:prime-number|indivisible whole number greater than $1$}} below the cutoff $x_1$, the allowed loss unit $\mathrm{loss}(p)$, and the combinatorial flags separating ordinary primes, primes with $p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$, exceptional primes, and colliding infinite places. This ledger is a 10-consumer hub shared across 6 stages of the proof, including Stage 02's sharpness proof (`02_derivatives/bound_attained`).
2. **Span the ramified source space by short columns.** To keep the output of the {{term:theta-correspondence|iterated theta lift}} small, the input forms on $X_0$ must already be integral and small under every symmetry of the {{concept:complex-plane|two-dimensional number system of real and imaginary parts}}. To control both properties at once, the proof checks each value against the {{concept:weyl-gauge|prime-by-prime integrality ruler that pulls a form's value back by the group element and requires every coordinate to have size at most $1$}}: a form that passes this test at every prime and keeps squared norm at most $B$ under every symmetry is called {{term:weyl-gauge|B-short}}. At a free level subgroup, {{term:weyl-gauge|B-short}} forms span the full source space. However, both {{concept:seesaw-duality|the symmetry equating an inner product on one pair of unitary groups with an inner product on a swapped pair}} and the $p = N$ divisibility argument require input forms to be *ramified* at every prime $w \mid \pi_N$ (where $\pi_N \bar{\pi}_N = N$): the sum of translates by powers of a local scaling generator $z_w$ of order $N - 1$ must vanish. Applying the product of difference operators $\prod_{w \mid \pi_N} (1 - z_w)$ enforces this vanishing simultaneously at all $f$ places above $\pi_N$. Each factor $(1 - z_w)$ keeps integrality and multiplies the squared-norm cap by at most $4$ (from $(1+1)^2 = 4$), so all $f$ places multiply $B$ by at most $4^f$, while the kernel discards at most a fraction $f / (N - 1) \le b^{-8}$ of the source space's dimension. Starting with $B = \exp(\mathrm{errorUnit}) / 4^f$ therefore yields a spanning set of $\exp(\mathrm{errorUnit})$-short {{term:weyl-gauge|ramified source forms}}.
3. **Cancel Stirling growth and Chowla–Selberg periods against the level $N$.** Each of the $m$ steps in the {{term:theta-correspondence|iterated theta lift}} evaluates a {{concept:schur-jet|multivariate Taylor expansion recording a form's partial derivatives at the origin}} of order $a$ at the chosen $a$-places and order $\kappa(\tau)$ at the background rows. By Stirling's bound for $\log a!$, one lift adds an archimedean size $s_\infty(a) = \log a! + a \log(M/\Omega)$. Meanwhile, the prime-by-prime gains $\mathrm{gain}_p$ sum on average to $s_{\mathrm{main}} = a(\log(a/\bar{\kappa}) + c_1)$. When we subtract $s_{\mathrm{main}}$ from $s_\infty(a)$, the Chowla–Selberg formula for the period $\Omega$ of the field with {{concept:complex-multiplication|extra arithmetic symmetries coming from an imaginary quadratic ring of integers}} cancels both Stirling's $-a$ and the transcendental constant $c_1$ identically. The remaining linear excess across all $m$ lifts is then canceled directly by restricting from $X_m$ to $Y_m$: restriction extracts a factor of $N^{-a}$ at each $a$-place, and the prime level $N$ is placed in a window designed so that $a \log N$ cancels $m(s_\infty(a) - s_{\mathrm{main}})$ up to $32 a \log b$ per $a$-place.
4. **Expand the glued input and project onto the good target part.** To make $p = N$ divisibility work, the composite lift takes a *glued* {{term:theta-correspondence|chain datum}} (`IsChainDatum`) that couples consecutive pairs of lifts modulo $N$. To bound the ordinary size at the trivial twist $\vartheta = 1$ (and to handle primes $p \neq N$), the proof expands that gluing into a finite sum of plain unglued vacuum translates—a *weak* {{term:theta-correspondence|chain datum}} (`IsWeakChainDatum`) with slightly broader support and norm budget that produces the exact same composite lift—and multiplies the single-step {{term:theta-correspondence|theta kernel}} bounds across all $m$ lifts. On the target side, cutting out the "good" representation piece multiplies by $|G| e_0 s$ for a finite matrix group $G$. Because $|G| e_0$ has algebraic-integer coefficients and shifts by integral elements, this good projection preserves integrality for *any* tame twist $\vartheta$, while at the trivial twist $\vartheta = 1$ it enlarges the class-sum squared norm by at most $\exp(x^{99})$.

### Where this analogy breaks

Describing $\prod_{w \mid \pi_N}(1 - z_w)$ as a "small filter" makes ramification sound like a minor technical cleanup. In fact, without forcing the sum of translates by $z_w^i$ ($0 \le i < N-1$) to vanish at every $w \mid \pi_N$, a source form can carry unramified central-character components that obstruct both {{term:seesaw-duality}} and $p = N$ divisibility. Nor is $\prod_{w \mid \pi_N}(1 - z_w)$ an orthogonal projection onto {{term:weyl-gauge|ramified source forms}}: it is a surjective endomorphism whose image equals that vanishing subspace and whose norm-expansion factor $4^f$ must be pre-paid inside the initial bound $\exp(\mathrm{errorUnit}) / 4^f$.

Likewise, saying that $s_\infty(a)$, $s_{\mathrm{main}}$, and $a \log N$ "cancel" does not mean the final archimedean exponent vanishes. The Chowla–Selberg identity cancels $c_1$ cleanly and the choice of $N$ cancels the leading $m a(\log \bar{\kappa} + \log(M \operatorname{rd}_E / 2\pi))$ term, but four pieces remain in the final exponent of `weylNormSq_compositeLift_le`: the background height $h_{\mathrm{main}}$, the prime-sum fluctuation term $-m \cdot \mathrm{rem}$, the explicit target $+2\sum \mathrm{gain}_p \log p$ (which cancels against the $p$-adic divisibility bounds in the {{term:arithmetic-height|product formula}}), and an error budget `errorUnit` $= f a (\log b)^3$ absorbing Stirling's $1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\log a$, row slacks, class counts, coefficient sums, and restriction slack.

Finally, proving the archimedean bounds only at the trivial twist $\vartheta = 1$ and via the unglued expansion `IsWeakChainDatum` does not mean twists or gluing are dispensable. Stage 01's {{term:twisted-lattice}} deliberately splits roles so that ordinary {{term:gram-determinant|covolumes}} are read at $\vartheta = 1$ while prime-by-prime indices are read at prime-avoiding twists $\vartheta$. Similarly, expanding a glued {{term:theta-correspondence|chain datum}} (`IsChainDatum`) into a plain `IsWeakChainDatum` forgets the modulo-$N$ gluing cancellation: the $N^{f m n_m / 2}$ expanded terms fit comfortably inside the archimedean budget $\exp(x^{60})$ and stay harmless at primes $p \neq N$, whereas at $p = N$ the proof must use the unexpanded gluing and the separate vacuum-projection bridge (`IsChainDatum.isWeakChainDatum`).

## Level 2 — Undergrad Bridge

### 1. Prime-by-prime divisibility ledger (`ShortColumns.lean`, a 10-consumer hub)

In `src/New/UpperBound/Size/ShortColumns.lean` (`lines 70–136`), the infinite places $D.\Phi$ of $E$ form the group $(\mathbb{Z}/q_1)^\times$, with $\Phi_a = D.\mathrm{aPlaces}$ the places $\equiv 1 \pmod{q_0}$, $\Phi_b = D.\mathrm{bPlaces}$ the designated places of class $-1 \pmod{q_0}$, and weight $w(\sigma) = D.\mathrm{weight}\ \sigma$ equal to $b$ on $\Phi_b$ and $a / x_1$ elsewhere. Writing $u(\nu) = D.\mathrm{placeOfInt}\ \nu$, $v(\pi_p) = \mathrm{piPrimeValuation}\ P.d\ p$ ($1/2$ if $p = d$, $1$ otherwise), and $I_p = \mathrm{positivePartnerExponents}\ P.d\ p$ (odd $e$ if $p$ is inert, all $e \ge 1$ if $p = d$, empty if $p$ splits), the file defines the prime-by-prime accounting quantities:

- **Local prime sum (`New.Data.localPrimeSum`):**
  $$s_p(y, \sigma) := v(\pi_p) \sum_{e \in I_p} \Bigl(\frac{y}{p^e} - w\bigl(u(-p^e)\sigma\bigr)\Bigr)_+.$$
- **Total weight sum (`New.Data.localPrimeSumTotal`) and requested gain (`New.Data.gain`):**
  $$F(p) := \sum_{\sigma \in D.\Phi} s_p\bigl(w(\sigma), \sigma\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{gain}(p) := m\Bigl(\sum_{\tau \in \Phi_a} s_p(a, \tau) + k\, F(p)\Bigr).$$
- **Prime classification and loss unit:**
  - `New.Data.IsSmallNonsplit p`: $p < x_1 \land \chi_K(p) \neq 1$.
  - `New.Data.IsPlusMinusOneModLifts p`: $m \mid (p - 1) \lor m \mid (p + 1)$, i.e., $p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$.
  - `New.Data.loss p`:
    $$\mathrm{loss}(p) := \frac{f a (\log b)^2}{p} \cdot \begin{cases} m & \text{if } p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod m, \\ 1 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
- **Exponents, order $o_0$, exceptional primes, and collisions:**
  - `New.Data.ceilLogLifts p`: $\mathfrak{r} := \lceil \log_p m \rceil$, the least natural number with $m \le p^{\mathfrak{r}}$, alongside `transferExponent` $\nu_p = \mathfrak{r} f (n_m + \mathfrak{r})$ and `collisionExponent` $j_p = \lceil 2 m f a / p \rceil$.
  - `New.Data.o₀ p`: the multiplicative order of the class of $-p$ reduced modulo $q_0$ in $(\mathbb{Z}/q_0)^\times$.
  - `New.Data.IsExceptional p`: $\chi_K(p) = -1$, $p \not\equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$, $o_0$ is odd, and $o_0 \le \mathfrak{r}$ (so at $e = o_0$, the place $u(-p^e)$ lands inside $\Phi_a$).
  - `New.Data.IsCollision p ev τ e` and `New.Data.collisionSum p ev`: an exponent $e \in I_p$ and place $\tau$ form a collision outside a finite exclusion set $\mathrm{ev} \subset D.\Phi$ when both $\tau$ and $u(-p^e)\tau$ lie in $\Phi_a \setminus \mathrm{ev}$; `collisionSum p ev` sums $a / p^e$ over all colliding pairs $(\tau, e)$.

Because these combinatorial quantities bridge all arithmetic stages of the proof, `03_upper_bound/short_columns` serves as a **10-consumer hub across 6 stages** in `dag/dag.json`: Stage 02 (`02_derivatives/bound_attained`, which proves that $F(p) = D.\mathrm{localPrimeSumTotal}\ p$ is attained), Stage 03 (`seesaw_identity`, `local_divisibility`, `archimedean_size`), Stage 04 (`04_spanning/spanning_theorem`), Stage 05 (`05_determinant/spanning_forms`), Stage 06 (`06_one_lift/three_exchanges`, `06_one_lift/twisted_value_heights`), and Stage 07 (`07_congruences/fit_twists_exist`, `07_congruences/twisting_theorem`).

### 2. $B$-short forms, scaling generators, and ramified source forms

In `src/New/UpperBound/Size/ShortColumns.lean` (`lines 157–1020`), an algebraic form $\varphi \in \mathrm{AlgForm}\ n\ \kappa\ e$ on the finite adelic unitary group $G_f(E, n)$ is **$B$-short** (`New.IsShort B φ`) if:
1. For every prime $p$ and every $p$-adic embedding $\iota_p : \mathbb{C} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}_p$, $\varphi$ is integral for Weyl's lattice (`GaugeLe 1 1 φ`), meaning `weylGauge κ e g (φ g) ≤ 1` for every $g \in G_f$; and
2. For every $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C})$ and every $g \in G_f$, the conjugated value satisfies `weylNormSq (σ ∘ φ g) ≤ B`.

**Spanning at a free level.** If $K \le \mathrm{stdLevel}\ E\ n$ is a free level ($\Gamma \cap g K g^{-1} = \{1\}$ for all $g \in G_f$), delta forms supported on single double cosets $\Gamma x K$ inherit shortness directly from their value vector $v$ at $x$ (`New.isShort_deltaForm`). Using evaluation vectors `weylEval` at integral arrays (`norm_weylEval_le_one_of_integral`, `weylRep_mul_weylRep_zero`), `norm_weylRep_mulVec_det_smul_weylEval_le_one` establishes $p$-adic integrality while `weylNormSq_det_smul_weylRep_mulVec_weylEval_le` bounds `weylNormSq` by $R^{6M}$. Choosing a nonzero integer $N_{\mathrm{diff}} \in \mathfrak{D}_{E/\mathbb{Q}}$ in the different ideal of $E$ gives **`New.span_isShort_eq_top`**: for any $M \ge n f + \mathrm{boxes}(\kappa, n)$ and
$$B \ge \bigl(6 N_{\mathrm{diff}} (f n)^2\bigr)^{6M},$$
the $B$-short forms span `levelSpace (algFormRep κ e) K`. Specializing to $X_0$ (rank $k$), $N_{\mathrm{diff}} = q_1 d$, and $\mathrm{boxes}(\kappa, k) \le f a$ (`sum_height_mul_columnCount_le`), **`New.Data.span_isShort_eq_top`** shows that $D.\mathrm{SourceForm}$ is spanned by $B$-short forms with
$$B = \frac{\exp(D.\mathrm{errorUnit})}{4^f}, \qquad D.\mathrm{errorUnit} = f a (\log b)^3.$$

**Ramifying at $\pi_N$ without losing dimension or shortness.** The level $D.\mathrm{levelX}\ j$ imposes `D.chosenLevel` together with the condition that at every $w \in D.\mathrm{piNPlaces}$ ($w \mid \pi_N$, where $\pi_N \bar{\pi}_N = N$ and $\#D.\mathrm{piNPlaces} = f$), the last row of $k_w - 1$ vanishes modulo $w$ (`mem_levelX`). At each $w \mid \pi_N$, `exists_isScalingAt_valuation_pow_iff` picks a **scaling generator** $z_w =$ `New.Data.scalingGenerator w` $\in U(1)(\mathbb{A}_f)$ (`IsScalingAt w z_w`) that is integral, equal to $1$ off $\{w, \bar{w}\}$, and generates $(\mathcal{O}_E / w)^\times$ of exact order $N - 1$:
$$v(z_w^i - 1) < 1 \iff (N - 1) \mid i \iff \mathrm{scalarFin}(X_j, z_w)^i \in D.\mathrm{levelX}\ j.$$
Hence $z_w$ acts on `levelSpace ... (D.levelX j)` through `algFormCentralRep` with $z_w^{N-1} = 1$ (`algFormCentralRep_scalingGenerator_pow`). By elementary linear algebra on an operator $T$ with $T^r = 1$ (`sum_pow_apply_eq_zero_iff_exists_eq_sub`, `iInf_ker_sum_pow_eq_range`), **`New.Data.sum_scalingGenerator_pow_eq_zero_iff_exists_eq_sub`** characterizes vanishing of the $(N-1)$-fold central average:
$$\sum_{i=0}^{N-2} z_w^i \varphi = 0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \exists \psi,\ \varphi = \psi - z_w \psi.$$
The subspace of **ramified source forms** is defined as the simultaneous vanishing kernel
$$D.\mathrm{ramifiedSourceForms} := \bigcap_{w \mid \pi_N} \ker\Bigl(\sum_{i=0}^{N-2} z_w^i\Bigr) = \mathrm{range}\bigl(D.\mathrm{ramifyingOp}\ \mathrm{univ}\bigr), \qquad D.\mathrm{ramifyingOp}\ s := \prod_{w \in s} (1 - z_w)$$
(`New.Data.ramifiedSourceForms_eq_range`). This ramification condition is essential downstream in [The Iterated Seesaw Identity](./seesaw-identity.md) and at $p = N$ in [Local Divisibility & Elementary Divisors](./local-divisibility-and-elementary-divisors.md), because expressing $\varphi = \psi - z_w \psi$ kills the unramified central-character modes at every $w \mid \pi_N$.

Two quantitative estimates show that passing to `ramifiedSourceForms` is nearly free:
1. **Dimension loss $\le f/(N-1) \le b^{-8}$:** Any form in $\ker(1 - z_w)$ is fixed by $K = D.\mathrm{levelX}\ 0 \sqcup \langle z_w \rangle \le D.\mathrm{chosenLevel}$, which is still free and satisfies $[K : D.\mathrm{levelX}\ 0] \ge N - 1$ (`N_sub_one_le_relIndex_levelX`). By `relIndex_mul_finrank_levelSpace`, $\dim \ker(1 - z_w) \le (N-1)^{-1} \dim D.\mathrm{SourceForm}$ (`finrank_ker_one_sub_scalingGenerator_le`). Subadditivity of kernel dimensions (`finrank_ker_mul_le`) across the $f$ factors yields `finrank_ker_ramifyingOp_le` and rank-nullity gives **`New.Data.le_finrank_ramifiedSourceForms`** and **`New.Data.f_div_N_sub_one_le`** (using $f \le b$ and $b^9 + 1 \le N$):
   $$\dim D.\mathrm{ramifiedSourceForms} \ge \Bigl(1 - \frac{f}{N-1}\Bigr)\dim D.\mathrm{SourceForm}, \qquad \frac{f}{N-1} \le b^{-8}.$$
2. **Shortness loss $\le 4^f$:** Because $k \in \mathrm{stdLevel}$ preserves `weylGauge` and $\sqrt{\mathrm{weylNormSq}}$ satisfies the triangle inequality, **`New.IsShort.sub_translate`** shows that if $\varphi$ is $B$-short then $\varphi - k \cdot \varphi$ is $4B$-short. Inducting over $w \in s$ yields **`New.Data.isShort_ramifyingOp`** ($4^{\#s} B$-short). Applying `ramifyingOp univ` (with $\#\mathrm{univ} = f$) to the $(\exp(\mathrm{errorUnit}) / 4^f)$-short spanning set from `New.Data.span_isShort_eq_top` gives **`New.Data.span_isShort_ramifiedSourceForms_eq_top`**:
   $$\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb{C}} \bigl\{\varphi \in D.\mathrm{ramifiedSourceForms} \mid \mathrm{IsShort}\ (\exp D.\mathrm{errorUnit})\ \varphi\bigr\} = \top.$$

### 3. Stirling + Chowla–Selberg cancellation and prime-sum rewriting (`ArchimedeanSize.lean`)

In `src/New/UpperBound/Size/ArchimedeanSize.lean` (`lines 71–187`), the archimedean jet size of a derivative of order $j$ is $s_\infty(j) = D.\mathrm{jetSize}\ j := \log j! + j \log(M/\Omega)$, where $M = D.M$ is the product of the two principal-level primes and $\Omega = P.\mathrm{bigOmega}\ D.\mathrm{towerExp}$ is the Chowla–Selberg period constant from Stage 02. The expected mean of the prime sums $s_{\mathrm{fin}}(a, \tau)$ over $\tau \in \Phi_a$ is $s_{\mathrm{main}} = D.\mathrm{mainPrimeSum} := a(\log(a/\bar{\kappa}) + c_1)$, where $\log \bar{\kappa} = \frac{1}{f}\sum_\tau \log \kappa(\tau)$ and $c_1 = \operatorname{Re}(\mathrm{logDerivMean}\ P.d\ 1\ 1)$.

**Exact cancellation of $c_1$ (`New.Data.jetSize_sub_mainPrimeSum_le`).**
The proof combines two inputs:
- Monotonicity of the Stirling sequence (`log_stirlingSeq'_antitone`) gives $\log a! \le a \log a - a + (1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\log a)$.
- Expanding `bigOmega` via `re_logDerivMean_one` gives $\log \Omega = -1 - c_1 + \log(2\pi / \operatorname{rd}_E)$, hence
  $$\log\frac{M}{\Omega} = 1 + c_1 + \log\frac{M \operatorname{rd}_E}{2\pi}.$$
Adding $\log a! + a \log(M/\Omega)$ and subtracting $a(\log a - \log \bar{\kappa} + c_1)$ cancels both $-a$ and $a c_1$ identically:
$$s_\infty(a) - s_{\mathrm{main}} \le a\bigl(\log \bar{\kappa} + G\bigr) + \Bigl(1 + \frac{\log a}{2}\Bigr), \qquad G := \log\frac{M \operatorname{rd}_E}{2\pi}.$$

**Level compensation (`New.Data.mul_jetSize_sub_mainPrimeSum_sub_le` & `card_aPlaces_mul_excess_le`).**
The prime level $N$ is chosen in `logLevelTarget` so that $\log N \ge m(\log \bar{\kappa} + G) - (G + \tfrac{15}{2}\log b)$. Multiplying `jetSize_sub_mainPrimeSum_le` by $m$ and subtracting $a \log N$ cancels the dominant term $m a (\log \bar{\kappa} + G)$:
$$m(s_\infty(a) - s_{\mathrm{main}}) - a \log N \le m\Bigl(1 + \frac{\log a}{2}\Bigr) + a\Bigl(G + \frac{15}{2}\log b\Bigr) \le a \log b + a(23 \log b + 8 \log b) = 32 a \log b,$$
using $m(1 + \tfrac{1}{2}\log a) \le a \log b$ and $|G| \le 23 \log b$. Summing over $\tau \in \Phi_a$ (with $\#\Phi_a \le f$) gives `card_aPlaces_mul_excess_le`:
$$\#\Phi_a\bigl(m(s_\infty(a) - s_{\mathrm{main}}) - a \log N\bigr) \le 32 f a \log b.$$

**Exact prime-sum rewriting (`two_mul_sum_gain_mul_log` & `mainAmount_sub_add_two_mul_sum_gain`).**
Because $\mathrm{gain}_p = 0$ at split primes, decomposing each prime sum `primeSum` into its `localPrimeSum` summands and interchanging the sums over $p$ and $\tau$ yields **`New.Data.two_mul_sum_gain_mul_log`**:
$$2 \sum_{p \in D.\mathrm{smallNonsplitPrimes}} \mathrm{gain}_p \log p = m \sum_{\tau \in \Phi_a} s_{\mathrm{fin}}(a, \tau) + m k \sum_{\tau \in D.\Phi} s_{\mathrm{fin}}(\kappa(\tau), \tau).$$
Recalling $h_{\mathrm{main}} = m k \sum_\tau (s_\infty(\kappa(\tau)) - s_{\mathrm{fin}}(\kappa(\tau), \tau))$ and $\mathrm{rem} = \sum_{\tau \in \Phi_a} (s_{\mathrm{fin}}(a, \tau) - s_{\mathrm{main}})$, substituting `two_mul_sum_gain_mul_log` cancels every $s_{\mathrm{fin}}$ term identically (**`New.Data.mainAmount_sub_add_two_mul_sum_gain`**):
$$h_{\mathrm{main}} - m\cdot \mathrm{rem} + 2 \sum_{p \in D.\mathrm{smallNonsplitPrimes}} \mathrm{gain}_p \log p = m \#\Phi_a s_{\mathrm{main}} + m k \sum_{\tau \in D.\Phi} s_\infty(\kappa(\tau)).$$

### 4. Row sizes, one-kernel norm bound, and `IsChainDatum` vs. `IsWeakChainDatum`

For lift $j < m$ from $X_j$ to $X_{j+1}$, `New.Data.rowSizeSum j` sums $s_\infty$ over the lengths of the first $n_j$ rows of the lift weight Young diagrams across all $\tau \in D.\Phi$, and `New.Data.chainSize` records the main term $m(\#\Phi_a s_\infty(a) + k \sum_\tau s_\infty(\kappa(\tau)))$. Every row satisfies `rowMainLength τ i + rowSlack i ≤ 2 a` (`rowMainLength_add_rowSlack_le`), so each box of slack costs at most $25 \log b$ (`sum_range_jetSize_add_eq`). Summing over $j < m$ absorbs the slack into half of `errorUnit` (**`New.Data.sum_rowSizeSum_le`**):
$$\sum_{j=0}^{m-1} \mathrm{rowSizeSum}\ j \le \mathrm{chainSize} + \frac{\mathrm{errorUnit}}{2},$$
while combining `mainAmount_sub_add_two_mul_sum_gain` with `card_aPlaces_mul_excess_le` gives **`New.Data.chainSize_sub_le`**:
$$\mathrm{chainSize} - \#\Phi_a a \log N \le h_{\mathrm{main}} - m\cdot \mathrm{rem} + 2 \sum_{p \in D.\mathrm{smallNonsplitPrimes}} \mathrm{gain}_p \log p + 32 f a \log b.$$

**One kernel and the restricted chain (trivial twist `TameChar.one`).**
At the **trivial twisting character `TameChar.one`**, **`New.Data.sqrt_weylNormSq_jetMap_le`** applies Stage 02's vacuum-jet bound (`sqrt_weylNormSq_schurJet_le_of_isVacuumCombination`) with growth parameter $a + 1$: for every $\sigma \in \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{C})$ and every vacuum translate `translateDatum t j`,
$$\sqrt{\mathrm{weylNormSq}\bigl(\sigma \circ (\mathrm{liftKernel}\ \mathrm{TameChar.one}\ j).\mathrm{jetMap}(\omega(g,g')(\mathrm{translateDatum}\ t\ j))\xi\bigr)} \le \exp\Bigl(x^{50} + \frac{\mathrm{rowSizeSum}\ j}{2}\Bigr) \sqrt{\mathrm{weylNormSq}(\sigma \circ \xi)}.$$
For a **weak chain datum** `hΨ : D.IsWeakChainDatum 1 Ψ` at the trivial twist, **`New.Data.weylNormSq_restrictedChain_le`** multiplies four bounds for `restrictedChain TameChar.one Ψ φ`:
1. The product of $m$ single-kernel bounds (`sqrt_weylNormSq_jetMap_le`), contributing $\prod_{j<m} \exp(x^{50} + \mathrm{rowSizeSum}\ j / 2) \cdot \sqrt{\exp B}$;
2. The class count across all $m$ intermediate spaces, at most $\exp(x^{30})^m$;
3. The conjugate $\ell^1$ coefficient bound $\sum_t \|\sigma(\Psi(t))\| \le \exp(x^{60})$ from `IsWeakChainDatum 1 Ψ`; and
4. The restriction bound `weylNormSq_restrictToY_le` from $X_m$ to $Y_m$, which introduces the level saving $-\bigl(\sum_\tau \mathrm{branchExponent}\ \tau\bigr)\log N \le -(\#\Phi_a a - f m \cdot \mathrm{halfGap})\log N$ up to $\exp(x^{70})$.

Packing $\exp(70 \log x) + f m \cdot \mathrm{halfGap} \log N + 2(m x^{30} + x^{60} + m x^{50}) + 32 f a \log b \le \mathrm{errorUnit}/2$ and adding `sum_rowSizeSum_le` + `chainSize_sub_le` gives the headline bound $\exp(B + h_{\mathrm{main}} - m\cdot \mathrm{rem} + 2\sum \mathrm{gain}_p \log p + \mathrm{errorUnit})$.

**`IsChainDatum` vs. `IsWeakChainDatum` (`ChainDatumExpansion.lean`) and `weylNormSq_compositeLift_le`.**
The two chain-datum predicates in `src/New/UpperBound/Divisibility/ChainDatumExpansion.lean` play distinct roles:
