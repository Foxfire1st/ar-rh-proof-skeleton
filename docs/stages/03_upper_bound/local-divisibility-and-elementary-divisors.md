## Level 1 — First-Principles

In Stage 02, differentiating an elliptic theta series at a single non-split {{concept:prime-number|whole number greater than $1$ with no smaller positive divisors}} $p$ produced an extra divisibility slope $\gamma_p$. Stage 03 turns that derivative calculation into a global arithmetic upper bound on a matrix of automorphic periods. To do that, we fix an embedding $\iota_p : \mathbb{C} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}_p$ from the {{concept:complex-plane|two-dimensional field of real and imaginary scalars}} $\mathbb{C}$ into the $p$-adic completion and inspect every prime $p$ one by one.

For a fixed prime $p$, we measure divisibility by powers of $p$ — the {{concept:arithmetic-height|exact prime-power divisibility exponent}} (the {{term:arithmetic-height|$p$-adic valuation}}) — across every coordinate of an automorphic form at once. That worst-case coordinate size is the form's {{concept:weyl-gauge|maximum prime-power size across all coordinates of an automorphic form}} (its {{term:weyl-gauge|$p$-adic gauge}}): saying a form has valuation at least $v$ means its {{term:weyl-gauge|$p$-adic gauge}} is at most $p^{-v}$. Stage 03 passes a source form through a {{concept:theta-correspondence|chain of $m$ successive lifting maps carrying source forms up through definite unitary spaces}} (an {{term:theta-correspondence|iterated theta lift}}, governed by a {{term:theta-correspondence|chain datum}} $\Psi$ for a tame twist $\vartheta$ of conductor prime to $p$) and restricts the output from $X_m$ to $Y_m$.

A single theorem, `exists_colengthDvd_gaugeLe_compositeLift`, delivers the same uniform output at every prime $p$: a submodule $S$ of the integral source forms $M$ whose index in $M$ divides a small prime power $p^l$ ($l \le \mathrm{allowedLoss}(p)\cdot\dim$), such that on every {{term:weyl-gauge|ramified source form}} in $S$, the composite lift has valuation at least $\mathrm{gain}(p) - \mathrm{allowedLoss}(p)$. The proof triages all primes $p$ into four cases controlled by the cutoff $x_1$, the level prime $N$, and the {{concept:quadratic-twist|sign $\chi_K(p)$ recording whether $p$ splits, stays inert, or ramifies in $K$}} ($\chi_K(p) \in \{+1, -1, 0\}$ for $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$):

1. **Split or large primes ($p \neq N$ with $\chi_K(p) = 1$ or $p \ge x_1$):** Nothing extra is demanded ($\mathrm{gain}(p) = 0$) and nothing is lost ($\mathrm{allowedLoss}(p) = 0$). Each individual lift preserves integrality, so we keep the full integral module $S = M$ with index exponent $l = 0$.
2. **Non-exceptional small non-split primes ($p < x_1$, $\chi_K(p) \neq 1$, not exceptional):** Two distinguished infinite places ($a$-places) can collide under the Stage 02 Galois shift, costing a collision penalty on each of the $m$ lifts. Outside the sparse exceptional primes, the single error budget $\mathrm{loss}(p)$ already pays for all $m$ lifts of collisions on the full module $S = M$, again with $l = 0$.
3. **Exceptional inert primes ($p < x_1$, $\chi_K(p) = -1$, $p \not\equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$, with odd order $o_0 \le \mathfrak{r}$):** Collisions between $a$-places are now too heavy to absorb on all of $M$. To eliminate them, we choose one prime from each conjugate pair above $p$ induced by the $a$-places (a full mark $\Lambda$) and form the product $Y$ of one {{concept:hecke-operator|finite averaging operator summing a form over adjacent lattices at a prime above $p$}} (each a {{term:hecke-operator|scaled Hecke operator}}) per prime $l \in \Lambda$. Passing $Y^m$ through the $m$ lifts cancels every collision on $Y^m M$, while multiplying directly by $p^{j_p}$ pays for all collisions crudely on $p^{j_p} M$. Taking the sum $S = Y^m M + p^{j_p} M$ achieves valuation at least $\mathrm{gain}(p) - 2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$ on $S$. What remains is to show that $S$ fills almost all of $M$ — equivalently, that the {{concept:elementary-divisor|diagonal prime-power factors controlling the index of a submodule inside a full lattice}} of $Y$ stay small outside a tiny fraction $\theta$ of coordinates, so the {{term:elementary-divisor|colength}} of $Y^m M + p^{j_p} M$ in $M$ is small.
4. **The level prime ($p = N$):** Because $x_1 \le N$, the requested gain is zero ($\mathrm{gain}(N) = 0$), and denominators at $N$ are traded for the level-raising operator $\mathrm{Up}^{m/2}$. Pulling back the submodule of forms whose $\mathrm{Up}^{m/2}$-image stays integral for the auxiliary lattice $L_0$ at the conjugate primes $\Sigma_N$ gives a module $S$ of {{term:elementary-divisor|colength}} exponent at most $m f b \cdot \dim \le 2 m f b \cdot \dim$ on which the composite lift loses at most $2 m f b$ powers of $N$.

![Four-case prime triage feeding into the edge-factored operator pipeline and submodule index bound](assets/stage03-four-case-divisibility-and-hecke-colength.svg)

Case 3 is the engine room of Stage 03, and it rests on a self-contained algebraic theorem in `ElementaryDivisorTheorem.lean`. At each marked prime above an exceptional inert prime $p$, the $a$-places inducing that prime or its conjugate arrange into a {{concept:frobenius-cycle|ring-homomorphism loop of $N$ alternating letters where each step raises entries to the $P$-th power modulo $p$}}. On this loop, the {{term:hecke-operator|scaled Hecke operator}} acts as an {{term:hecke-operator|edge Hecke operator}} summing over cosets of a two-block {{term:hecke-operator|parahoric subgroup}} (edges between lattices).

How do you prove that an operator $A$ on a huge lattice is almost surjective modulo $p^m$ — that its {{term:elementary-divisor|elementary divisors}} have small average exponent outside a small share $\theta$? Think of $A$ as a three-step pipe:
$$M \subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}} \subseteq M_{\mathrm{edge}} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Add}} \mathcal{N} \subseteq M^\circ.$$
First, restrict each vertex function to all outgoing edges ($M \subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}} \subseteq M_{\mathrm{edge}}$). Second, pair each edge function with dual-edge functions and sum over incoming edges ($\mathrm{Add} : M_{\mathrm{edge}} \to \mathcal{N}$). Third, embed $\mathcal{N}$ back into the dual vertex lattice $M^\circ$. The two outer inclusions $M \subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}}$ and $\mathcal{N} \subseteq M^\circ$ have small, directly computable determinant losses. In the middle, $\mathrm{Add}$ is far from square (there are many more edges than vertices), yet we construct an explicit {{term:elementary-divisor|near inverse}} $\mathrm{Inv}$ whose outputs satisfy strict congruences modulo $w^c$ — an {{term:frobenius-cycle|edge condition}} enforced by averaging away monomials above a {{term:frobenius-cycle|defect threshold}} — such that $\mathrm{Add} \cdot \mathrm{Inv} \equiv p^{2 N c_0} I \pmod{w^c}$. Over the residue field, {{term:frobenius-cycle|Stepanov's count}} of zeros against degree bounds the dimension of the space of edge functions obeying those congruences to at most $(1 + \theta)$ times the vertex dimension. Putting $M_{\mathrm{all}}$ and the image of $\mathrm{Inv}$ into {{term:elementary-divisor|Smith normal form}} relative to $M_{\mathrm{all}}$ and applying Nakayama's lemma shows that at most a $\theta$-fraction of coordinates can fail to be hit up to $p^{2 N c_0}$, bounding the {{term:elementary-divisor|colength}} of $A(M) + p^m M$ in $M$.

One subtle ring-theoretic fact shapes every definition in both files: the coefficient ring $O$ modeling $p$-integral scalars in $\mathbb{C}$ (under $\iota_p : \mathbb{C} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}_p$) is a characteristic-zero **valuation domain**, not a discrete valuation ring like $\mathbb{Z}_p$. Because its value group is dense ($\mathbb{Q}$) and it is non-noetherian, a quotient $M / S$ need not have finite module length, whereas the square root $w = \sqrt{p}$ already lives inside $O$. That is why Stage 03 measures submodule indices by determinant-divisibility (`ColengthDvd`) instead of module length.

### Where this analogy breaks

Two simplifications in the pipe picture require care in the real proof. First, `ColengthDvd M S (p^l)` is not literally a finite quotient cardinality $|M / S|$: over the non-discrete, non-noetherian valuation domain `embValRing`, ideals are not generated by a single uniformizer, so the theorem certifies instead that every finite family of vectors in $M$ can be combined into $S$ by an $O$-matrix whose determinant divides $p^l$. Second, the {{term:elementary-divisor|near inverse}} $\mathrm{Inv}$ does not invert $\mathrm{Add}$ modulo a power of $p$ directly on all functions; it works modulo $w^c$ (with $w^2 = p$) only against forms in the halfway lattices of all edges (`allEdgesForms`), and because $O$ is not discrete, the Nakayama elimination must step down by one factor of $w$ from $w^c$ to $w^{c-1}$ so that $p^{2 N c_0} w \mid w^{c-1}$.

## Level 2 — Undergrad Bridge

### 1. Valuation domains, `ColengthDvd`, and `IsOntoUpToColength`

Fix a prime $p$ and a chosen embedding $\iota_p : \mathbb{C} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}_p$ (`[PadicPlace p]`). Write `embValRing` for the subring $\{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid \|\iota_p(z)\| \le 1\}$. It is a valuation domain of characteristic zero: for any two elements $x, y \in O$, either $x \mid y$ or $y \mid x$, so finite matrices over $O$ admit a Smith normal form (`isValuationPair_top.smith_normal_form`). However, because $\iota_p(\mathbb{C})$ contains algebraic numbers of arbitrary ramification over $\mathbb{Q}_p$, `embValRing` is neither discrete nor noetherian.

Accordingly, `New.Reduction.Lattices.Colength` defines two basis-free, length-free predicates over any commutative ring $O$:

- **`ColengthDvd M N z`:** For every finite family $\Phi : \mathrm{Fin}\,n \to V$ in $M$, there exists an $n \times n$ matrix $m$ over $O$ with $\det m \mid z$ such that every row combination $\sum_k m_{ik} \Phi_k$ lies in $N$. It is transitive (`ColengthDvd.trans`, multiplying the bounds), pulls back along linear maps (`ColengthDvd.comap`), pushes forward (`ColengthDvd.map`), and satisfies `ColengthDvd.of_det_dvd` for square matrices over a valuation domain.
- **`IsOntoUpToColength p M n y c`:** An endomorphism $y$ preserves the $O$-submodule $M$ ($y(M) \subseteq M$), and there exists $l \in \mathbb{N}$ with $(l : \mathbb{R}) \le c$ such that `ColengthDvd M (y(M) + p^n • M) (p^l)`. Crucially, the upper bounds $c$ **add** under products of endomorphisms (`IsOntoUpToColength.prod`).

Over any valuation domain $O$ of characteristic zero in which $p$ is a non-unit and admits a square root $w \in O$ with $w^2 = p$, the entire elementary-divisor argument works directly over $O$, specializing immediately to `embValRing`.

### 2. The 4-case prime triage (`LocalDivisibility.lean`)

Recall the Stage 03 prime parameters for admissible data $D : \mathrm{Data}\,P$ (with $m = D.m$ lifts, $f = D.f = |\Phi|$ infinite places of $E$, long-row length $a = D.a$, weight parameter $b = D.b$, cutoff $x_1 = D.x_1$, and level prime $N = D.N \ge x_1$):

- **`gain p`:** the target valuation of the $m$-step composite lift at $p$ (zero whenever $\chi_K(p) = 1$ or $p \ge x_1$).
- **`loss p`:** $f a (\log b)^2 / p$, multiplied by $m$ if $p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$.
- **`allowedLoss p`:** $2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$ if $p < x_1$ and $\chi_K(p) \neq 1$ (`smallNonsplitPrimes`); otherwise $2 m f b$ if $p = N$, and $0$ otherwise.
- **`collisionSum p ev`:** $\sum a / p^e$ over collisions $(\tau, e)$ between $a$-places outside `ev`.
- **`collisionExponent p`:** $j_p = \lceil 2 m f a / p \rceil$.
- **`IsExceptional p`:** $\chi_K(p) = -1$, $p \not\equiv \pm 1 \pmod m$, and the multiplicative order $o_0 = D.o_0(p)$ of $-p \pmod{q_0}$ is odd and at most $\mathfrak{r} = \mathrm{ceilLogLifts}\,p = \lceil \log_p m \rceil$.

Away from $p = N$, every chain datum $\Psi$ for a twist $\vartheta$ expands via `exists_isWeakChainDatum_compositeLift_eq` into a weak chain datum $\Psi'$ with `compositeLift ϑ Ψ = restrictedChain ϑ Ψ'`. The gauge helpers (`GaugeLe.rpow_mono`, `.add`, `.smul`, `.zpow_neg_smul`) and `gaugeLe_restrictedChain_of_mem_raises` then assemble the four cases of **`New.Data.exists_colengthDvd_gaugeLe_compositeLift`**:

| Case | Prime Condition | `gain p` | `allowedLoss p` | Submodule $S \subseteq M$ | Colength exponent $l$ | Key Valuation / Colength Lemmas |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | $p \neq N$ and ($\chi_K(p) = 1$ or $p \ge x_1$) | $0$ | $0$ | $M$ | $0$ | `gaugeLe_restrictedChain_one` (uses $p \neq d$, so standard data have ratio $1$) |
| **2** | $p < x_1$, $\chi_K(p) \neq 1$, $\neg\mathrm{IsExceptional}\,p$ | $\mathrm{gain}(p)$ | $2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$ | $M$ | $0$ | `mul_collisionSum_le_loss` ($m\cdot\mathrm{collisionSum}\,p\,\emptyset \le \mathrm{loss}(p)$) + `gaugeLe_restrictedChain_collisionSum` |
| **3** | $p < x_1$, $\mathrm{IsExceptional}\,p$ (inert) | $\mathrm{gain}(p)$ | $2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$ | $Y^m M + p^{j_p} M$ | $\le \mathrm{loss}(p)\dim \le 2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)\dim$ | `exists_isFullMark`, `gaugeLe_restrictedChain_of_isFullMark`, `gaugeLe_pow_collisionExponent_smul`, `gaugeLe_compositeLift_of_isFullMark`, `isOntoUpToColength_prod_scaledHeckeOperator_pow` |
| **4** | $p = N$ (hence $x_1 \le N$) | $0$ | $2 m f b$ | pullback of `upIntegralForms` | $\le m f b \dim \le 2 m f b \dim$ | `ColengthAtLevel.lean::exists_colengthDvd_upIntegralForms_pow`, `ValuationAtLevel.lean::gaugeLe_compositeLift_at_N` |

Walking through the mechanics of **Case 3** and **Case 4**:

- **Case 3 (Exceptional inert $p < x_1$):** Since $p$ is exceptional, `exists_isFullMark` picks a finite set $\Lambda$ of primes of $E$ above $p$ disjoint from its conjugate $\bar\Lambda$ such that every $a$-place induces a prime in $\Lambda \cup \bar\Lambda$ (`IsFullMark`). For each $l \in \Lambda$, `scaledHeckeOperator l` is $p^{A_l} T_{l, \mathfrak{r}}$ where $A_l = \mathrm{heckeScalingExponent}(l) = a \cdot |\{\tau \in a\text{-places} \mid \mathrm{inducedPlace}\,\tau = l\}|$. Let $Y = \prod_{l \in \Lambda} \mathrm{scaledHeckeOperator}(l)$.
  - *Valuation on $Y^m M$:* `ValuationAtNonSplitPrimes.lean::gaugeLe_scaledHeckeOperator_pow` pushes one factor of $Y$ through each of the $m$ lifts onto the theta data, eliminating all collisions (`collisionSum_placesInducing_eq_zero`). At each step $j < m$, the surplus $a / x_1$ at each $a$-place inducing $\Lambda$ plus $\mathrm{loss}(p) / m$ pays for the low columns and transfer exponents (`card_mul_transferExponent_add_sum_lowColumns_le`), so summing over $j < m$ proves **`gaugeLe_restrictedChain_of_isFullMark`**: valuation $\ge \mathrm{gain}(p) - 2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$ on $Y^m M$.
  - *Crude valuation on $p^{j_p} M$:* Since every collision has exponent $e \ge 1$, `collisionSum p ∅ ≤ 2 f a / p`, so $m \cdot \mathrm{collisionSum}\,p\,\emptyset \le j_p = \lceil 2 m f a / p \rceil$. Scaling by $p^{j_p}$ pays for all $m$ lifts of collisions at once (`gaugeLe_pow_collisionExponent_smul`), giving valuation $\ge \mathrm{gain}(p) - \mathrm{loss}(p) \ge \mathrm{gain}(p) - 2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)$.
  - *Combining the two pieces:* Ultrametricity (`GaugeLe.add`) gives **`gaugeLe_compositeLift_of_isFullMark`** on $S = Y^m M + p^{j_p} M$.
  - *Colength of $S$ in $M$:* For each $l \in \Lambda$, `MarkOperator.lean::isOntoUpToColength_scaledHeckeOperator` invokes `ElementaryDivisorTheorem.lean::elementary_divisors` (with $\theta = 1 / (m^2 f)$ and $3 c_{\mathrm{div}} \le 12 A_l / (m p)$) to show that $\mathrm{scaledHeckeOperator}(l)$ is onto modulo $p^{j_p}$ up to colength exponent $(12 A_l / (m p) + j_p / (m^2 f)) \cdot \dim$. Multiplying over $m$ copies of $\Lambda$ (`IsOntoUpToColength.prod`) and applying `mul_sum_heckeScalingExponent_le_loss` (using $\sum_{l \in \Lambda} A_l \le a f$ and $|\Lambda| \le f$) bounds the total colength exponent by $\mathrm{loss}(p)\cdot\dim \le 2\cdot\mathrm{loss}(p)\cdot\dim$ (**`isOntoUpToColength_prod_scaledHeckeOperator_pow`**).
- **Case 4 (Level prime $p = N$):** Because $x_1 \le N$, `gain N = 0`. In `ColengthAtLevel.lean`, comparing three integral modules at $\Sigma_N$ (standard lattice $\mathcal{A}_\iota$, auxiliary lattice $\mathcal{A}^L$ at $\delta_0 = \mathrm{diagPointN}\,0$, and dual lattice $\mathcal{A}^\vee$ at $\delta_0^\vee = \mathrm{dualPointN}\,0$) bounds the colength of `upIntegralForms` ($\mathcal{A}'$, the preimage of $\mathcal{A}^L$ under $\mathrm{Up}^{m/2}$) in $\mathcal{A}_\iota$ by $N^l$ with $l \le m f b \cdot \dim$ (`exists_colengthDvd_upIntegralForms_pow`). On **ramified** source forms (`φ ∈ D.ramifiedSourceForms`, the only place Case 4 uses ramification), `ValuationAtLevel.lean::gaugeLe_compositeLift_at_N` applies the split-level lift law to express the glued chain as a bounded scalar times the direct chain after $\mathrm{Up}^{m/2}$, losing at most $2 m f b$ powers of $N$.

### 3. Frobenius cycles, edge coordinates, and factoring `edgeHeckeOperator` (`ElementaryDivisorTheorem.lean`)

Fix a local ring $W$ with finite residue field of size $q = P^N$, a characteristic-zero valuation domain $O$ in which $p$ is not a unit, and a **Frobenius cycle** $T : \mathrm{FrobeniusCycle}\,N\,W\,O$:
- a prime $p = T.p$ with a square root $w = T.\mathrm{sqrtPrime} \in O$ ($w^2 = p$),
- a prime power $P = p^{\mathrm{frobExp}} \ge 5$,
- $N$ ring homomorphisms ("letters") $\iota_s : W \to O$ ($s \in \mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z}$), alternating between even and odd (`isEven_succ`), satisfying $\iota_{s+1}(x) \equiv \iota_s(x)^P \pmod p$.

Let $k$ be the rank, $n$ the degree in each letter, $r$ the first block size ($0 < r \le k/2$), and $\theta \in (0, 1]$ the allowed fraction of exceptional elementary divisors. Monomials of degree $n$ in $k$ variables per letter are indexed by `Exponents N k n`. A matrix $g \in \mathrm{GL}_k(W)$ acts on forms by $\rho(g) = T.\mathrm{letterRep}\,n\,g$ (using $\iota_s(g)$ at even letters and $\iota_s(g^{-\top})$ at odd letters). The `depth` of a monomial $\beta$ counts its degree in the deep variables (indices $\ge r$ at even letters and $< r$ at odd letters), giving diagonal matrices $D = \mathrm{depthScaling}$ ($w^{\mathrm{depth}\,\beta}$) and $D^2 = \mathrm{depthPower}$ ($p^{\mathrm{depth}\,\beta}$).

The structure **`New.EdgeCoordinates W O k r X H`** abstracts the Hecke graph in coordinates:
- $X$ represents the finite set of lattice orbits; $H$ indexes a basis of the auxiliary free $O$-module at each vertex; edges into $x \in X$ are pairs $e = (x, b) \in X \times \mathrm{ParahoricCosets}\,W\,k\,r$, where `parahoric W (twoBlock k r)` is the two-block parahoric subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}_k(W)$.
- Each edge carries `origin e : X`, adapted basis changes `originBasisChange e : GL (Fin k) W` and `terminusBasisChange b : GL (Fin k) W`, and a module transition `transition e : GL H O`.
- The condition `mk_terminusBasisChange_inv` pins `(terminusBasisChange b)⁻¹` to coset $b$, and `eq_of_origin_eq` encodes freeness of the group action — which immediately implies `EdgeCoordinates.surjective_origin_originBasisChange` (every origin–coset pair occurs as an edge).

For an edge pairing `hb : T.IsEdgePairing b b'` — weights $b, b' : \mathrm{Exponents}\,N\,k\,n \to O$ with $b \cdot b' = p^{N c_0}$ ($c_0 = T.\mathrm{multinomialExponent}\,k = k^2 \log_p q$) such that $\rho(g)$ and $\rho(g^\top)$ are adjoint for $(h, h') \mapsto h' \cdot (b h)$ — the identity `transpose_mul_diagonal_mul` rewrites each edge's matrix:
$$(D \rho(\gamma))^\top \operatorname{diag}(b) (D \rho(g^\top)) = (\rho(g) D^2 \rho(\gamma))^\top \operatorname{diag}(b).$$
Summing over edges proves **`IsEdgePairing.edgeHeckeOperator_regroup`**: under `regroup J`, the operator `edgeHeckeOperator n J 𝓔` applied to $b$-weighted vectors factors as
$$M \subseteq M_{\mathrm{all}} \subseteq M_{\mathrm{edge}} \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Add}\;} \mathcal{N} \subseteq M^\circ,$$
namely `pairingMap 𝓔.transition b (edgeRestriction_dual) * edgeRestriction_origin`, where `edgeRestriction` restricts vertex forms to edges in the adapted basis scaled by $D$, and `pairingMap u b Q'` (`Add`) pairs edge values with dual-edge restrictions using `transition` on $O^H$ and `b` on forms.

### 4. Edge conditions, Stepanov's count, averaging, and Pieri cone volume (`exists_isEdgeCondition`)

Under the standing size hypothesis **`T.IsLargeDegree k n r θ`**:
$$0 < r \le k/2, \qquad 0 < \theta \le 1, \qquad n^4 \le q, \qquad \left(\frac{N k \log q}{\theta}\right)^5 P^{3r} \le n,$$
set the congruence precision $c = \mathrm{congruencePrecision}\,k = 5 N c_0$ and defect threshold $c^* = \mathrm{defectThreshold}\,k\,c = 3 k c$. Theorem **`New.FrobeniusCycle.exists_isEdgeCondition`** constructs a triple $(C, S, \mathrm{Av})$ satisfying `IsEdgeCondition` by assembling three upstream modules:

1. **Stepanov's count and extreme defect terms (`RootLemma.lean`):** `New.stepanov_count` proves that if a nonzero polynomial $\Phi \in R[X]$ vanishes to order $E$ at the image of every residue class of $W$ under $\iota : W \to R$, then $E \cdot |\mathrm{ResidueField}\,W| \le \deg \Phi$. Applying this along $\mathrm{SL}_2(W)$ embedded in two rows/columns of $\mathrm{GL}_k(W)$ yields `defect_nonpos_of_extreme_term`: any term of minimal $w$-adic order plus Gauss weight across all $\mathrm{SL}_2(W)$-translates has non-positive defect along the root.
2. **The averaging map (`AveragingMap.lean`):** `exists_average_mem_defectConditions_and_dvd_sub` defines $C = T.\mathrm{defectConditions}\,n\,a\,r\,(w^c)\,(3 c^*)$ (forms whose Levi translates have all coefficients of defect $> 3 c^*$ divisible by $w^c$). Using Levi-invariant linear forms on generic matrix polynomials applied one copy per letter, it constructs $\mathrm{Av}$ whose columns lie in $C$ (`average_mem`), while against every $h' \in T.\mathrm{allEdgesForms}\,k\,n\,r$ (forms in the halfway lattices of all edges from a vertex),
   $$w^c \;\Big|\; h' \cdot (b \cdot \mathrm{Av}\,h) - p^{N c_0}\, h' \cdot (b \cdot h)$$
   (`pow_dvd_pairing_average`). Meanwhile `allEdgesForms_le_defectConditions` guarantees $T.\mathrm{allEdgesForms}\,k\,n\,r \le C$.
3. **Pieri cone volume and residue-field dimension (`DefectCount.lean`):** Set $S$ to be the defect-condition subspace over `ResidueField O` (zero ideal, threshold $3 c^*$). Dividing out $w^{c-1}$ (`mem_translatesIn_of_pow_smul_mem`) shows that whenever $w^{c-1} h \in C$, the reduction of $h$ modulo the maximal ideal lies in $S$ (`residue_mem`). Packing Frobenius powers (`powerPacking`) and mapping minimal-height Levi-translate exponents into the Pieri cone via full flags (`exists_finrank_defectConditions_le_volume`, using `RootLemma.lean`), `index_mul_finrank_defectConditions_le` (under `mul_defectThreshold_le_mul_degree`: $8 N k c^* P^{2r} \le \theta n$) proves the dimension bound:
   $$[\mathrm{GL}_k(W) : P_{r, k-r}] \cdot \dim_{\mathrm{Res}(O)} S \;\le\; (1 + \theta)\cdot |\mathrm{Exponents}\,N\,k\,n|.$$

### 5. Near inverse, Smith normal form, Nakayama, and final colength bound

From `exists_isEdgeCondition`, the proof of **`New.FrobeniusCycle.elementary_divisors`** completes in five algebraic steps:

1. **Basis factorization at both ends (`exists_basis_det_dvd`, used twice):** Because every origin–coset pair occurs among both the edges and the dual edges, `exists_originFunctions_factorization_det_dvd` and `sum_finrank_deepForms_le` factor both `edgeRestriction` maps as $Q d$ and $Q' d'$ where $R Q = 1$, $R' Q' = 1$, the columns of $Q, Q'$ span `originFunctions` (lying in `valuedIn _ allEdgesForms`), and
   $$\det d \;\Big|\; w^{|V| D}, \qquad \det d' \;\Big|\; w^{|V| D}, \qquad D = \lfloor c_{\mathrm{div}} |\mathrm{Exponents}| \rfloor,$$
   where $c_{\mathrm{div}} = T.\mathrm{divisorConstant}\,k\,n\,r = N n \bigl(P^{-2r} + r^2 / (k P)\bigr)$. Since $w^2 = p$, the two ends combine to $\det(d'^\top)\det(d) \mid p^{|V| D}$.
2. **Near inverse (`pairingMap_mul_nearInverse_one`, `pow_dvd_mul_sub_smul_one`):** Define $\mathrm{Inv} = \mathrm{nearInverse}\,u\,\mathrm{Av}\,b'\,R'$. Without $\mathrm{Av}$ (setting $\mathrm{Av} = 1$), $b \cdot b' = p^{N c_0}$ and $R' Q' = 1$ give $\mathrm{Add}\cdot\mathrm{nearInverse}\,u\,1\,b'\,R' = p^{N c_0} I$. Inserting $\mathrm{Av}$ and summing the edgewise congruence `pow_dvd_pairing_average` yields
   $$w^c \;\Big|\; (\mathrm{Add}\cdot\mathrm{Inv} - p^{2 N c_0} I)_{i, j}.$$
