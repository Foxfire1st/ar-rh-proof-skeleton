# Stage 04 — Spanning: Second Moment (Theorem B)

Stage 04 supplies the missing half of the upper side of [Stage 01](../01_reduction/overview.md)'s main inequality: **Theorem B** (`D.enough_forms`). In [Stage 03](../03_upper_bound/overview.md), the seesaw identity and definite theta lifts produced source forms whose lifted images are both short in archimedean size and heavily divisible at every small non-split prime—yet Stage 03 deliberately stopped short of proving that those lifted images are nonzero or span a large subspace of the target space. Stage 04 closes that gap by proving a sharp second-moment estimate on the Poincaré map $P$: almost no energy is lost when projecting onto the good target forms, the sum of squared overlaps across the finite level group $\bar{G} = G_{p_0} \times G_{p_1}$ nearly hits the theoretical Welch floor, and consequently one good translate always captures a fixed fraction of any uncovered part of the target space (`New.Data.isSpread_goodPoincare`).

## Level 1 — First-Principles

Think back to the three-way tension driving [Stage 01](../01_reduction/overview.md). Theorem A ([Stage 03](../03_upper_bound/overview.md)) produces lifted target vectors that are extraordinarily short once their prime-power divisibility is divided out. Theorem C ([Stage 05](../05_determinant/overview.md) and Stages 06–07) sets an arithmetic floor on the {{concept:gram-determinant|squared parallelotope volume of any spanning family of integral target vectors}}. If every short vector from Theorem A pointed along the same line—or collapsed to zero altogether—its volume would be zero and no contradiction could ever follow. To trigger the cancellation in `star_inequality`, the proof must guarantee that a small collection of group translates of the Poincaré map spreads out across almost the entire target space of dimension $d_T = D.\mathrm{targetDim}$.

Stage 04 solves this spanning problem through a four-stage analytic funnel:

1. **Counting the target space without touching it directly (`equal_multiplicities`):** The target forms live on an *indefinite* unitary space $Y_0$, where non-compactness prevents counting $d_T$ directly. By projecting onto good forms with a {{concept:central-idempotent|orthogonal projection built from finite-field polynomial labels including at least one primitive polynomial}}, every local test function becomes cuspidal at a split finite place. That unlocks {{concept:trace-comparison|matching traces of commuting averaging operators between two spaces that share all finite primes and differ only in real signature}} from $Y_0$ to the *definite* space $W_m$, where the congruence level acts freely and total dimension is an exact product $\#\bar{G}\cdot \mathrm{deg}\cdot \mathrm{mass}_r$.
2. **Unfolding overlaps into block matrices (`moments`):** Measuring how two translates $P$ and $\rho(s) P$ overlap in {{concept:hilbert-schmidt-norm|root-sum-of-squares Frobenius operator norm}} unfolds $P_s = P^* \rho(s) P$ into class-indexed sums of {{concept:bessel-orbital-integral|archimedean correlation integrals over double cosets}}. Near the Bessel subgroup $\bar{R}_0$, the operator $P_s$ closely tracks an explicit unitary {{term:bessel-orbital-integral|Bessel translate}} $r_\psi(s)$, forcing $P^* P \approx 1$. Factoring the level across *two* distinct {{concept:prime-number|primes}} $p_0, p_1$ lets Stage 04 bound the bad-type projection loss one prime at a time, proving that the good-forms map $e_0 P$ is a {{term:hilbert-schmidt-norm|near isometry}} (error $5 b^{-4}$).
3. **Splitting the whole-group second moment into two analytic engines (`energy_estimate`, `remaining_terms_sum`):** Across all $s \in \bar{G}$, generic elements in the big Bruhat cell are bounded with sharp leading constant $1 + b^{-4}$ by the {{concept:kloosterman-sieve|inductive one-sided Poisson summation sieve pairing archimedean band-limited kernels with finite Kloosterman densities}}. Every cross-link pair, degenerate determinant term, and non-open reduction is crushed far below the main term using {{concept:link-lattice|common-link sublattices counted via Siegel's lemma and a weighted Schur test}}.
4. **Welch-style spreading and greedy covering (`spanning_theorem` $\to$ Stage 01):** Multiplying the target-dimension ceiling $d_T \le \#\bar{G}\cdot \mathrm{deg}\cdot \mathrm{mass}_r$ by the whole-group second moment $\sum_{s \in \bar{G}} \|P^* s P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le (1 + 9 b^{-4}) D_X^2 / (\mathrm{deg}\cdot \mathrm{mass}_r)$ cancels $\mathrm{deg}\cdot \mathrm{mass}_r$ identically, yielding
   $$d_T \sum_{s \in \bar{G}} \|P^* s P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le \#\bar{G}\,(1 + 9 b^{-4})\, D_X^2.$$
   This nearly saturates the {{term:hilbert-schmidt-norm|Welch bound}}. Cauchy–Schwarz in the operator space forces the {{term:hilbert-schmidt-norm|spreading property}} `New.Data.isSpread_goodPoincare`: against any subspace $F$ occupying share $\alpha$ of the target space, some translate $e_0 s P$ adds at least roughly $\frac{1 - \alpha}{2} D_X$ fresh dimensions. In [Stage 01](../01_reduction/overview.md), Wolsey's {{term:hilbert-schmidt-norm|greedy covering}} lemma (`FewTranslates.lean`) repeats that step $O(\log b \cdot d_T / D_X)$ times to span at least $(1 - 4/b^2) d_T$ dimensions (`D.enough_forms`).

### Where this analogy breaks

Describing Stage 04 as "proving that shifted copies of one subspace spread evenly across a bigger space like an almost-tight frame" captures the exact Hilbert–Schmidt Cauchy–Schwarz mechanism in `SpanningTheorem.lean` and `FewTranslates.lean`, but conceals where 85 of the 90 upstream Lean files live. The linear-algebraic spreading step itself takes only two files (`FewTranslates.lean` and `SpanningTheorem.lean`); virtually all of the analytic weight sits underneath it in proving that the unfolded Poincaré operators actually achieve $(1 + 9 b^{-4})\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{main}}$ with sharp leading constant $1$ rather than a loose constant $C$ or $X^\varepsilon$. Any constant factor above $1$—even $2$—would destroy the AM–GM spreading threshold $\frac{1 + \alpha}{2} + O(\varepsilon)$ needed for greedy covering to converge.

Tracing the exact quantitative chain across the five landmarks:

1. **Multiplicity equality (`Multiplicities/EqualMultiplicities.lean`, 17 decls):**
   For any two Hermitian spaces $V, V'$ of rank $r$ for whose signatures `D.paramW` has gap `D.gap`, every good label family $c \in D.\mathrm{goodTupleFamilies}$ carries a primitive degree-$r$ polynomial at some chosen split place (`exists_isPrimitivePolynomial`). Its local idempotent $e_c$ yields a cuspidal test function, so `trace_heckeOnGood_eq` equates $\operatorname{tr}(T|_{D.\mathrm{goodForms}\ V}) = \operatorname{tr}(T|_{D.\mathrm{goodForms}\ V'})$ for all $T \in D.\mathrm{heckeAlgebra}$. Consequences: equal characteristic polynomials and determinants (`charpoly_heckeOnGood_eq`, `det_heckeOnGood_eq`), equal eigensystem multiplicities (`finrank_goodForms_inf_eigenPart_eq`), equal Rallis determinants (`det_rallisOnGood_eq`, consumed by `05_determinant/norm_of_chain`), and $D.\mathrm{targetDim} = \dim_{\mathbb{C}}(D.\mathrm{goodForms}\ W_j)$ (`targetDim_eq_finrank_goodForms_spaceW`).
2. **Main-term big-cell sieve (`MainTerm/EnergyEstimate.lean`, 15 decls, 3 stub truncations):**
   Open reductions $s \in \bar{G}$ force every rational term $\gamma$ over $(c, s, c')$ to have nonvanishing proper leading frame minors (`IsOpenElement.leadingMinor_ne_zero`). Siegel's step (`IsFillingData.mul_sum_norm_sum_cosetAmplitude_sq_le`) pairs the archimedean band-limited Bessel Gram matrix (`prod_bandLimit_eq_gram`) against finite-place Kloosterman densities `G.lastEntryDensity / Y.massRatio`, with the global product formula canceling intermediate head powers (`gramScalar_mul_headDensity`). Entry-by-entry one-sided Poisson summation (`GlobalSieve.lean::sieve`) and constant assembly (`prod_poissonConst_eq`, `sieve_constant_le`) prove `linkSecondMoment_isOpenElement_le`:
   $$\mathrm{linkSecondMoment}\ \{\gamma \mid \mathsf{g}(\gamma) \neq 0\}\ \{s \in S.\mathrm{fixer}\ S_g\ D \mid S.\mathrm{IsOpenElement}\ s\} \le (1 + b^{-4})\, b^{-6k|S_g|}\, \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{main}}\, \nu^2.$$
3. **Remaining-term link-lattice bounds (`RemainingTerms/Sum.lean`, 26 decls):**
   For near pairs $(\gamma, \gamma')$ over an agreeing block $(c, s, c')$, `pairDiscriminant_sq_le` gains $(2/M)^{2if}$ in the common-link discriminant $d$, while `corank_le_and_pairDiscriminant_le` keeps corank $l \le n^3$ and $d \le b^{n^3 f}$. Combining the weighted Schur test (`SchurTest.lean`) with Bombieri–Vaaler Siegel's lemma (`SiegelLemma.lean`) crushes all cross-link and $\mathsf{g} = 0$ pairs to $\le b^{-kf} \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{id}}$ (`sum_termSize_mul_not_inSameLinkClass_le` $\Rightarrow$ `secondMoment_remainingTerms_le`), and crushes same-link $\mathsf{g} \neq 0$ pairs over reductions with `meetNorm s ≥ z` to $\le (b^{2f}/z)^k \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{main}}$ (`sum_termSize_mul_inSameLinkClass_le` $\Rightarrow$ `linkSecondMoment_le_of_le_meetNorm`).
4. **Unfolding and the two moments (`Unfolding/Moments.lean`, 40 decls):**
   Under `HasAmplitudeBlocks`, the scaled Poincaré map $P$ satisfies:
   - near-$\bar{R}_0$ error $\le b^{-12} \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{id}}$ (`sum_hilbertSchmidtNorm_sub_besselTranslate_sq_le`) $\Rightarrow$ $\|P^* P - 1\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le b^{-12} D_X$ (`hilbertSchmidtNorm_sub_one_sq_le`);
   - whole-group second moment $\sum_{s \in \bar{G}} \|P_s\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le (1 + 9 b^{-4}) \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{main}}$ (`sum_hilbertSchmidtNorm_sq_le`);
   - two-prime bad-type projection loss $\|(1 - e_0) P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le 4 b^{-4} D_X$ (`hilbertSchmidtNorm_one_sub_piTensorElem_comp_sq_le`, using `sum_norm_reducedCharMean_sq_le`: $\sum |\psi_U^\natural(s)|^2 \le 2$), yielding `isNearIsometry_piTensorElem_comp` with error $5 b^{-4}$.
5. **Target dimension and spreading (`SecondMoment/SpanningTheorem.lean`, 21 decls):**
   Counting principal-level forms on the definite space $W_m$ gives $d_T \le \#\bar{G}\cdot \mathrm{deg}\cdot \mathrm{mass}_r$ (`targetDim_le_card_mul_besselDegree_mul_mass`), so $d_T \sum_{s \in \bar{G}} \|P^* s P\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2 \le \#\bar{G}(1 + 9 b^{-4}) D_X^2$ (`targetDim_mul_sum_hilbertSchmidtNorm_sq_le`). Combining this with `isNearIsometry_e₀_comp_scaledPoincare` (error $5 b^{-4} \le \varepsilon_0 = (8 b^2)^{-1}$) inside `FewTranslates.lean::isSpread_of_second_moment` yields **`New.Data.isSpread_goodPoincare`** (`IsSpread D.goodPoincare (3 / 2 * (8 * D.b ^ 2)⁻¹)`), which Stage 01 converts into Theorem B (`D.enough_forms`) via `IsSpread.exists_few_le_finrank_iSup_range`.
