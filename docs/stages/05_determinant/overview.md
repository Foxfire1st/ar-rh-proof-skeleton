# Stage 05 — Lower Bound: The Determinant (Theorem C, Part 1)

Stage 05 supplies the lower-bound pillar of [Stage 01](../01_reduction/overview.md)'s main cancellation inequality: **Theorem C** (`New.Data.integral_forms_long`). While [Stage 03](../03_upper_bound/overview.md) (Theorem A) and [Stage 04](../04_spanning/overview.md) (Theorem B) push the covolume plus twice the index of lifted target forms *down* from above, Stage 05 proves that any linearly independent family spanning at least $(1 - 4/b^2)d$ of the $d$-dimensional target space must have covolume plus twice its index *at least* $d \cdot (D.\mathrm{mainAmount} - 3 f a \log^4 b)$. Together with [Stage 06 (`06_one_lift`)](../06_one_lift/overview.md) and Stage 07 (`07_congruences`), Stage 05 plays a **double architectural role**: it builds the core theta-exchange, square-commutation, determinant-factorization, and twist-interface machinery exported to Stages 06–07, and it then brings Stage 06 and Stage 07 back together inside `LongForms.lean` to assemble Theorem C and hand it directly into Stage 01's `New.star_inequality`.

## Level 1 — First-Principles

In [Stage 01](../01_reduction/overview.md), the whole proof of GRH boils down to trapping the prime-counting error inside a balance scale. Theorems A and B ([Stage 03](../03_upper_bound/overview.md) and [Stage 04](../04_spanning/overview.md)) produce $n \ge (1 - 4/b^2) d$ linearly independent target forms whose lifted images are remarkably short, driving their combined volume down whenever the prime remainder $R_{q_0}(x)$ is large. For that downward push to produce a contradiction, something must hold the floor rigidly from below. That floor is **Theorem C**, powered by {{concept:arithmetic-height|the global product formula stating that a nonzero algebraic number cannot simultaneously be tiny in ordinary size and divisible by huge prime powers}} inside Stage 01's {{concept:twisted-lattice|twisted-lattice height formalism}}.

To push the floor up to the full baseline $d \cdot D.\mathrm{mainAmount}$, Stage 05 feeds four coordinated inputs into Stage 01's `TwistedLattice.covolume_add_index_nonneg`:

1. **Complementary exchange and commuting squares (`theorem_for_partners`, `one_square`):** Whenever two Hermitian spaces have {{concept:siegel-weil-exchange|complementary ranks summing to the rank of a middle space $W$}}, their theta integrals over matched finite-prime matrix data agree up to a nonzero scalar $c$ whose modulus $\|c\|^2$ is governed by {{concept:hecke-l-value-ratio|partial Hecke L-values}}. Doubling $W = U_1 \oplus U_2$ via {{concept:seesaw-duality|seesaw pairing}} turns that exchange into the {{concept:theta-square-law|commuting theta square}}: going up-then-down through a larger space $A$ agrees with going down-then-up through a smaller space $A'$ up to $\kappa / c$, where $\kappa$ is independent of the twist.
2. **Factoring the $m$-step chain volume (`norm_of_chain`):** The target space consists of good forms on the indefinite space $Y_0$, mapped into $Y_m$ by an $m$-step chain $\Theta_\vartheta$. Using the {{term:theta-square-law}} across a {{concept:rallis-inner-product|triangle of lifts}}, Stage 05 slides every lift past every descent until $\Theta_\vartheta^* \Theta_\vartheta$ becomes a scalar multiple of a product of $m$ single-step {{term:rallis-inner-product|Rallis inner product}} operators on $Y_0$. Borrowing Stage 04's {{concept:trace-comparison|signature transfer of Rallis determinants}} (`det_rallisOnGood_eq`), Stage 05 factors the global chain {{concept:gram-determinant|Gram determinant}} ratio $Z(\vartheta)/Z(1)$ into the product of $m$ single-lift ratios $Z_j(\vartheta^j)/Z_j(1)$ times a controlled scalar built from $\binom{m}{2}$ {{term:hecke-l-value-ratio|quotient ratios}}.
3. **Completing almost-spanning bases cleanly (`spanning_forms`):** Because Theorem B leaves up to $n' = d - n \le 4d/b^2$ dimensions uncovered, Stage 05 constructs a spanning supply of {{concept:auxiliary-spanning-form|auxiliary spanning forms}} on $Y_0$. Sweeping a monotone {{term:auxiliary-spanning-form|two-grid path}} across $m^2$ squares transfers period integrals from the lower chain to explicit short deep-level forms on a definite space, where testing against dual-weight {{term:auxiliary-spanning-form|delta forms}} controls both {{concept:weyl-gauge|p-adic integrality}} and archimedean norm. Even though each auxiliary form costs $2 x_1^2 b^2 + b$, multiplying by $n' \le 4d/b^2$ cancels $b^2$ and caps the total {{term:auxiliary-spanning-form|completion cost}} at $d b$.
4. **Contracting regular twists and assembling Theorem C (`fit_twists`, `long_forms`):** Stage 05 specifies the exact contract of a {{concept:regular-twist-family|regular twist family}} $\theta(\iota)$—one {{term:regular-twist-family|m-fold twist}} for each {{concept:prime-number|prime}} embedding $\iota : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, with modulus coprime to $\ell$, {{term:regular-twist-family|small ratios}} of height at most $x_1$, and lift/projection denominators summing within the {{term:regular-twist-family|denominator budget}} $f a \log^3 b / (m k)$. Taking two typed black boxes unpacked in Stages 06 and 07—
   - **[Stage 06 (`06_one_lift/lift_volume_height`)](../06_one_lift/lift-volume-height.md):** *Input:* a {{term:regular-twist-family}} $\theta$ and lift index $1 \le j \le m$. *Output:* a sharp height bound $\le d(f a \log^4 b / m - k \cdot \mathrm{directionAmount})$ on the single-lift determinant ratio $Z_j(1)/Z_j(\theta(\iota)^j)$ (`heightLe_liftDeterminant_ratio`).
   - **Stage 07 (`07_congruences/fit_twists_exist`, typed black box):** *Input:* admissible data $D$. *Output:* existence of a {{term:regular-twist-family}} $\theta$ (`exists_isRegularFamily`).
   —`LongForms.lean` adds the $m$ single-lift heights (so $m \cdot k \cdot \mathrm{directionAmount} = D.\mathrm{mainAmount}$), combines them with the auxiliary completion cost inside `TwistedLattice.covolume_add_index_nonneg`, and proves **Theorem C** (`New.Data.integral_forms_long`):
   $$d \cdot \bigl(D.\mathrm{mainAmount} - 3 f a \log^4 b\bigr) \le c + 2\delta.$$

### Where this analogy breaks

Presenting Stage 05 as "factoring a chain determinant into $m$ pieces and padding a partial basis with cheap filler vectors" captures the high-level arithmetic of `LongForms.lean`, but hides two profound difficulties. First, the **chain** $\Theta_\vartheta : Y_0 \to Y_m$ leaves the convergent range of the classical Rallis inner product formula after the very first lift, so you cannot compute $\det(\Theta_\vartheta^* \Theta_\vartheta)$ directly—Stage 05 has to build an entire ramified Siegel–Weil exchange theorem (`TheoremForPartners.lean`) and square commutation calculus (`OneSquare.lean`) just to slide lifts past descents across a triangle of spaces, and even then the square law allows $\kappa = 0$ until Rallis injectivity proves positivity separately. Second, the auxiliary vectors are *not* individually short (their squared norm exponent is $2 x_1^2 b^2$, vastly larger than $b$); their total completion cost stays below $d b$ only because Theorem B's uncovered fraction $4/b^2$ kills the $b^2$ factor quadratically.

Across the 6 landmarks, the quantitative chain runs in two halves that converge inside `LongForms.lean`:

$$\begin{aligned}
\texttt{theorem\_for\_partners} \;\Longrightarrow\; \texttt{one\_square} &\;\Longrightarrow\; \begin{cases} \texttt{norm\_of\_chain} \text{ (with \texttt{equal\_multiplicities}, \texttt{fit\_twists})} \\ \texttt{spanning\_forms} \text{ (with \texttt{short\_columns}, \texttt{fit\_twists})} \end{cases} \\
\texttt{norm\_of\_chain} + \texttt{spanning\_forms} + \underbrace{\texttt{lift\_volume\_height}}_{\text{Stage 06}} + \underbrace{\texttt{fit\_twists\_exist}}_{\text{Stage 07}} &\;\Longrightarrow\; \texttt{long\_forms} \text{ (Theorem C)} \;\Longrightarrow\; \texttt{main\_inequality}.
\end{aligned}$$

1. **Complementary exchange (`Exchange/TheoremForPartners.lean`, 27 decls, 3 truncations):**
   For Hermitian spaces $W$ (rank $|a|+|b|$, $q$ negative signs), $V$ (rank $|a| = |b| + 2e$), and $V'$ (rank $|b|$) satisfying $2q + 2 \le |n| \le 2\mu + 2q$, `New.Exchange.exchange_identity` constructs $C > 0$ and a real Schwartz vector $x$ (before $\mathfrak{m}, \nu$ are chosen) such that on every tame $(\mathfrak{m}, \nu, \chi)$ and matched pair $(\Phi, \Psi)$ modulo $\mathfrak{m}$, the theta integral over $[U(V)]$ equals $c$ times the integral over $[U(V')]$, with $\|c\|^2 = C \cdot L_*(e) \neq 0$.
2. **Commuting theta square (`Exchange/OneSquare.lean`, 32 decls, 5 truncations):**
   Applying `exchange_identity` to $W = U_1 \oplus U_2$ paired against $A$ (rank $|n|+2t$) and $A'$ (rank $|n|$) yields `one_square_identity_of_isEdgeFamily`: up-then-down $\Theta_i$ and down-then-up $\Theta'_i$ satisfy `SquareLaw (χ i) σ (κ / c)` with $\|c\|^2 = C \cdot L_*(t) \neq 0$ and $\kappa$ independent of $i$ (possibly zero at this stage).
3. **Chain determinant factorization (`Chain/NormOfChain.lean`, 24 decls, 1 truncation):**
   Tiling the triangle of lifts with `OneSquare` gives $\Theta_\vartheta^* \Theta_\vartheta = c(\vartheta) c_Q^m R_\vartheta$ (`exists_adjoint_chain_comp_eq_smul_rallisProduct`), where positivity $C > 0$ comes from `chainDeterminant_pos`. Combining `exists_normDet_goodLift_sq_eq` with Stage 04's `det_rallisOnGood_eq` yields `exists_chainDeterminant_pos_and_div_chainDeterminant_one_eq`:
   $$\frac{Z(\vartheta)}{Z(1)} = \Bigl(\mathrm{Nm}(Q)^{e/2}\prod_{1 \le i < j \le m} \mathrm{quotientRatio}_{i,j}(\vartheta)\Bigr)^d \prod_{j=1}^m \frac{Z_j(\vartheta^j)}{Z_j(1)},$$
   while `HasSmallRatios.heightLe_inv_rpow_mul_prod_quotientRatio` bounds the inverse scalar's height by $m^2 t x_1 \le b$.
4. **Two-grid auxiliary spanning forms (`AuxiliaryForms/SpanningForms.lean`, 29 decls, 0 truncations):**
   Executing $m^2$ turns across two square grids $Y^a_b, \widetilde{Y}^b_a$ (`DiagonalTurns.lean`) transfers first-path pairings to `lastPathPeriod` (`exists_integral_lowerChainUpTo_eq_lastPathPeriod`), whose denominators clear by $\exp(x^{99} b^2)$ and conjugates grow by at most $\exp(x^{200} b^2)$. Reading values with dual-weight delta forms (`exists_dualPairing_eq_mul_lastPathPeriod`) and lifting short deep-level forms (`span_isShort_eq_top_gridLevelForms`) produces `exists_span_short_lifts_eq_top` (bound $\exp(x^{202} b^2)$).
5. **Regular twist interface (`Twist/FitTwists.lean`, 17 decls, 0 truncations):**
   Defines `CriticalPair.ratio`, `IsRamified`, `IsMainPair`, `denominatorBudget` ($f a \log^3 b / (m k)$), `ClearsProjection`, `HasDenominatorsLe`, `IsDenominatorBound`, `HasSmallRatios`, and `IsRegularFamily`, serving 7 consumers across Stages 05, 06, and 07.
6. **Theorem C assembly (`Twist/LongForms.lean`, 9 decls, 0 truncations):**
   Projects `exists_span_short_lifts_eq_top` to `exists_span_isAuxiliaryForm_eq_top` (size $\exp(2 x_1^2 b^2)$, squared turn-ratio scalar height $2 m^2 t x_1 \le b$), combines Stage 07's `exists_isRegularFamily` and Stage 06's `heightLe_liftDeterminant_ratio` to get `HeightLe (d (b + f a log⁴ b - mainAmount))` on $Z(1)/Z(\theta_\iota)$, applies `TwistedLattice.covolume_add_index_nonneg`, and uses `mul_completion_cost_le` ($n'(2 x_1^2 b^2 + b) \le d b$) plus $2 d b \le 2 d f a \log^4 b$ to prove `New.Data.integral_forms_long`:
   $$d \cdot \bigl(D.\mathrm{mainAmount} - 3 f a \log^4 b\bigr) \le c + 2\delta.$$
