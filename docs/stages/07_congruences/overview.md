# Stage 07 — Lower Bound: Congruences & Galois Representations (Theorem C, Part 3)

Stage 07 is the eighth and final pillar of the proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and the Riemann Hypothesis. In [Stage 05](../05_determinant/overview.md) and [Stage 06](../06_one_lift/overview.md), Theorem C (`New.Data.integral_forms_long`) was proved conditional on a single typed black box: the existence of a regular family $\theta$ of $m$-fold anticyclotomic twists (`D.IsRegularFamily θ`) whose inverse partial $L$-value ratios have arithmetic height at most $x_1$ (`HasSmallRatios`) and whose theta-lift projection denominators sum across all primes within `D.denominatorBudget = f a log³ b / (m k)` (`IsDenominatorBound`). Stage 07 discharges that final black box unconditionally in **`New.exists_isRegularFamily`** (`07_congruences/fit_twists_exist`), sealing the entire 8-pillar architecture back through [Stage 05](../05_determinant/regular-twists-and-theorem-c.md) (`05_determinant/long_forms`) and [Stage 01](../01_reduction/main-inequality.md) (`New.star_inequality`) to the five headline theorems of [Stage 00](../00_statement/main-theorems.md).

## Level 1 — First-Principles

Why is constructing {{concept:regular-twist-family|a regular twist family}} $\theta$ so demanding? At every {{concept:prime-number|prime}} $\ell$ and field isomorphism $\iota : \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$, Stage 07 must exhibit one {{term:regular-twist-family|m-fold twist}} $\tau = \theta(\iota)$ whose modulus $Q$ is coprime to $\ell$ and whose first $m$ powers $\vartheta = \tau^j$ ($1 \le j \le m$) keep *two* arithmetic obstacles simultaneously small:

1. **No large $L$-value denominators (`HasSmallRatios`):** Every {{concept:hecke-l-value-ratio|ramified partial Hecke L-value ratio}} $p.\mathrm{ratio}(\vartheta, \pm 1)$ must stay barely divisible by $\ell$ so its inverse has {{concept:arithmetic-height|global arithmetic height}} at most $x_1$.
2. **No large congruence denominators (`IsDenominatorBound`):** When an $\ell$-integral modular form on $U(V_j)$ satisfying {{concept:false-lift-period|false-lift orthogonality}} is projected orthogonally onto the subspace $Z$ of theta lifts from $W_j$ (or onto the line of a rank-one {{concept:character-theta-lift|character theta lift}}), congruences modulo powers of $\ell$ between lifted and non-lifted eigenforms can create denominators. We must produce an operator clearing those denominators whose determinant loss sums across all primes $\ell$ within the {{term:regular-twist-family|denominator budget}} $f a \log^3 b / (m k)$.

Stage 07 builds $\theta(\iota)$ independently at each pair $\iota = (\ell, \mathbb{C} \simeq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)$ by assembling four interlocking arithmetic machines across six landmark files:

1. **Local $p$-adic Hodge lifting and uniform local loss (`cup_orthogonality_bounds`, `crystalline_defect`):** Congruences produce torsion Galois cocycles modulo $\ell^M$ that need not lift to crystalline cocycles when Hodge–Tate weights exceed $\ell - 1$. Using {{concept:kummer-chart-cup-orthogonality|Frobenius-orbit safe-move walks on a Kummer chart}} across four prime regimes—including a rank-independent bound that directly consumes Stage 01's Behrend sparsity of `D.bPlaces` and a tame-chart descent at the level primes $p_0, p_1$—Stage 07 bounds every summand's {{concept:crystalline-dual-defect|crystalline dual defect}} uniformly by $[G_\ell : \Gamma]\cdot \mathrm{rank}(L_c)\cdot \mathrm{defectBound}(D, \ell) / x$.
2. **Twist-independent Galois blocks from Hecke eigensystems (`rows_from_eigensystems`):** Factoring the split-place {{concept:hecke-operator|Hecke polynomial}} of any {{concept:theta-correspondence|theta lift}} splits its attached Galois trace into a twist $\psi_\vartheta$ of a fixed, twist-independent {{concept:galois-main-summand|Galois main summand}} $T$ plus the {{term:galois-main-summand|inverse-cyclotomic string}} $\sum_{e=0}^{t-1} \chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-e}$. Because the space of good forms cut out by the {{concept:central-idempotent|good finite-field idempotent}} $e_0$ is finite-dimensional, there are only finitely many rank-$r$ and rank-$1$ main summands to control simultaneously.
3. **Simultaneous $m$-fold Selmer twisting (`twisting_theorem`):** Packaging those finitely many {{term:galois-main-summand|main summands}} as {{concept:anticyclotomic-selmer-twisting|Selmer data}} alongside {{term:anticyclotomic-selmer-twisting|Stickelberger systems}} built from Stage 06's {{concept:carrier-form|three-exchange class-sum measures}} (`06_one_lift/three_exchanges`, via `Selmer/ClassSums.lean`), the horizontal {{term:anticyclotomic-selmer-twisting|twisting theorem}} finds one $m$-fold twist $\tau$ whose first $m$ powers simultaneously bound auxiliary ramification, all {{term:anticyclotomic-selmer-twisting|divisible crystalline Selmer groups}}, and all ramified $L$-value ratio valuations by `twistError ℓ D` $= (4f + 3)r v_\ell(D.\mathrm{chosenGroupOrder}) + 2^{-\ell}$.
4. **Bypassing Vandiver's conjecture, clearing denominators, and closing the budget (`denominator_bound`, `fit_twists_exist`):** To clear projection denominators without ever needing Vandiver's conjecture, Stage 07 uses the Mazur–Wiles bound (`oddClassBound`) for odd cyclotomic cohomology classes and constructs an upper-triangular {{concept:taylor-wiles-prime-triangle|Taylor–Wiles prime triangle}} whose Kummer reciprocity laws eliminate all {{term:taylor-wiles-prime-triangle|even Tate-twist classes}} algebraically while keeping {{term:taylor-wiles-prime-triangle|weak-lift root chains}} intact. This yields a {{concept:ribet-clearing-operator|Ribet clearing operator}} $\Lambda$ with per-rank exponent `clearingBound D ℓ`. Summing `(clearingBound D ℓ + v_ℓ(|StdClasses|)) * log ℓ` over all primes fits strictly inside $D.\mathrm{denominatorBudget}$, proving **`New.exists_isRegularFamily`** and closing the complete 8-pillar chain.

**Terminology nuances from upstream `GUIDE.md` §2:** Reading the Stage 07 Lean sources requires tracking five conventions that differ from casual mathematical shorthand:
- *"Fit" means regular:* the top-level file is named `FitTwistsExist.lean` (matching Stage 05's `FitTwists.lean`), whereas the Lean predicate it discharges is `D.IsRegularFamily θ`.
- *"Lifting" in `New/Congruences/Lifting/` means $p$-adic Hodge lifting:* `CupOrthogonalityBounds.lean` studies lifting torsion Galois cocycles to crystalline cocycles via Kisin modules—not theta lifting (which appears as `goodLift` / `trivialLift`).
- *"Row" denotes a main summand:* in `New/Congruences/`, a "row" is an $\ell$-adic Galois representation satisfying `IsMainSummandLike` (in earlier stages, "row" also denotes a target form).
- *"String" denotes inverse-cyclotomic powers:* inside `HasLiftTrace`, a "string" of length $t$ is the sum $\sum_{e=0}^{t-1} \chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-e}$ accompanying the twisted main summand.
- *Independent per-embedding choices:* `New.exists_isRegularFamily` chooses $\theta(\iota)$ independently for each `ι : PrimePadicIso` (using `Classical.choose` on `exists_mFoldTwist_hasDenominatorsLe`); distinct embeddings above the same prime $\ell$ need not pick the same twist, because every bound depends only on the rational prime $\ell$ and therefore sums cleanly across primes.

### Where this analogy breaks

Summarizing Stage 07 as "pick a twist that kills congruences at each prime $\ell$" hides two essential realities. First, at primes dividing $M = p_0 p_1$ or $D.\mathrm{chosenGroupOrder}$ or small primes $\ell \le 2b$, neither the local crystalline defect nor the twisted Selmer group vanishes; Stage 07 accepts controlled nonzero losses at those primes and relies on the scale hierarchy $a = xb \gg m^2 e^{180} b$ so their $\log\ell$-weighted sum stays strictly below $D.\mathrm{denominatorBudget} = f a \log^3 b / (m k)$. Second, even for odd primes away from the level, the even Tate-twist cohomology classes (`evenClasses`) are never bounded in size—only algebraically neutralized by the upper-triangular prime triangle `IsPrimeTriangle`.
