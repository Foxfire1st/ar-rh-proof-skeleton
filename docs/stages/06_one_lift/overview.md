# Stage 06 — Lower Bound: One Lift (Theorem C, Part 2)

Stage 06 supplies the negative-height engine inside Theorem C: computing the exact **cost of a single theta lift** (`New.Data.heightLe_liftDeterminant_ratio`). In [Stage 05](../05_determinant/overview.md), `NormOfChain.lean` factored the global $m$-step chain determinant ratio $Z(1)/Z(\xi_\iota)$ into $m$ single-lift ratios $Z_j(1)/Z_j(\xi_\iota^j)$ ($1 \le j \le m$), and `LongForms.lean` reduced Theorem C to bounding each single-lift ratio from above by a strongly negative height. Stage 06 discharges that contract in full—breaking the naive $2/3$ exponent barrier (Alpöge Steps 14, 15, and 18) and delivering the $-d \cdot k \cdot D.\mathrm{directionAmount}$ term per lift that sums over $j = 1, \dots, m$ to $-d \cdot D.\mathrm{mainAmount}$ inside [Stage 05 — Regular Twist Families and the Assembly of Theorem C](../05_determinant/regular-twists-and-theorem-c.md) (`05_determinant/long_forms`).

## Level 1 — First-Principles

In [Stage 05](../05_determinant/overview.md), we saw how the lower bound of Theorem C splits into $m$ single-step ratios $Z_j(1) / Z_j(\xi_\iota^j)$, where $Z_j(\vartheta)$ is the {{concept:gram-determinant|squared parallelotope volume (Gram determinant)}} of the $j$-th theta lift $\Theta_{j,\vartheta}$ from the rank-$r$ Hermitian space $W_j$ to the rank-$(r + t)$ definite space $V_j$ ($t = 5r$). If you bound each lift naively without twisting and gluing, the extra "unused" dimensions of $V_j$ inflate the volume so badly that the resulting prime-counting bound stalls at an exponent of $2/3$ instead of reaching $1/2$.

Stage 06 overcomes that $2/3$ barrier through {{concept:idle-direction-repayment|an exact negative-height accounting that repays all $k = r + 1 - m$ idle directions per lift}} across {{concept:regular-twist-family|a regular twist family}} $\theta$ (treated here as a typed black-box input whose existence is unpacked in Stage 07). Four coordinated constructions turn one single lift into a negative {{concept:arithmetic-height|global arithmetic height}}:

1. **Three exchanges (`three_exchanges`):** Starting from Stage 05's {{concept:siegel-weil-exchange|complementary-rank exchange identity}}, Stage 06 runs three successive exchanges until the entire twist dependence collapses into a single scalar $c(\vartheta)$ in front of the {{concept:carrier-form|fixed, nonzero, full-level holomorphic theta lift $\varphi^\circ$ on $U(2m, m)$}} and twist-free functions $\mathsf{F}_i$ on $U(W_j)$. The squared norm $\|c(\vartheta)\|^2 = C / \Gamma_j(\vartheta)$ is controlled by the raw {{term:carrier-form|Hecke value product}} $\Gamma_j(\vartheta)$ over three {{term:carrier-form|exchange pairs}}.
2. **Gluing across split blocks (`gluing_maps`):** Splitting $V_j = V'' \oplus V^\circ$ (ranks $r + 9m + 1$ and $t - 9m - 1$) along congruence-glued lattices yields {{concept:divided-lattice-gluing|the linear gluing map $\mathrm{glue}_\vartheta$ pairing a twist-carrying first factor on $V''$ with arbitrary forms on $V^\circ$}}. By {{concept:seesaw-duality|seesaw adjoint transfer}}, its adjoint satisfies $c'_\vartheta\,\mathrm{glue}_\vartheta^*\,\Theta_{j,\vartheta} = \Theta^\circ$ with $\Theta^\circ$ completely independent of $\vartheta$. Injectivity of $\Theta^\circ$ paired with [Stage 04](../04_spanning/overview.md)'s guarantee that the target space is nonzero proves $c'_\vartheta \neq 0$ (and retroactively certifies $C \neq 0$).
3. **Vanishing periods of false lifts and negative $L$-value heights (`periods_of_false_lifts`, `twisted_value_heights`):** To keep projections onto the lift subspace clean, Stage 06 climbs a {{concept:false-lift-period|hyperbolic-plane descent tower}} proving that every complementary-block period of a false lift vanishes whenever $2r + 2N + 3 < n$. That feeds two consumers at once: it proves glued forms are orthogonal to false lifts on $V_j$, and it validates the {{concept:character-theta-lift|four-block seesaw test forms}} on $U(2z)$ that pair with rank-one lifts of {{concept:hecke-l-value-ratio|main-pair}} characters. Combining the untwisted lift norm with [Stage 02](../02_derivatives/overview.md)'s sharpness theorem (`bound_attained`) gives each main pair the negative height $6\,\mathrm{denominatorBudget} - D.\mathrm{directionAmount}$, and aggregating all $k/2$ squared main pairs plus $5m$ squared ramified pairs gives $\rho_\vartheta^{-1} = \Gamma_j(1)/\Gamma_j(\vartheta)$ height at most $10 m x_1 + k(6\,\mathrm{denominatorBudget} - D.\mathrm{directionAmount})$.
4. **Comparison operator and single-lift volume height (`lift_volume_height`):** Assembling $U_\vartheta = n_{\mathrm{Aut}}\,\mathrm{hk}^2\,\mathrm{glue}_\vartheta \mathrm{glue}_\vartheta^*$ via a {{concept:tight-frame|tight frame}} of integral tuples builds {{concept:lift-comparison-operator|a self-adjoint lift comparison operator}} whose compression onto $\mathrm{range}(\Theta_{j,\vartheta})$ satisfies $Z_j(\vartheta)\Delta(\vartheta) = C \rho_\vartheta^d$. Dividing untwisted by twisted cancels $C$ identically:
   $$\frac{Z_j(1)}{Z_j(\xi_\iota^j)} = \frac{\Delta(\xi_\iota^j)}{\Delta(1)} \cdot \rho_{\xi_\iota^j}^{-d}.$$
   Bounding $\Delta(1)$ at infinity and $\Delta(\xi_\iota^j)$ at every {{concept:prime-number|prime}} $\ell$ via `ClearsProjection`, adding the negative height of $\rho_{\xi_\iota^j}^{-d}$, and absorbing all positive error terms into $k \log b \cdot \mathrm{denominatorBudget}$ proves **`New.Data.heightLe_liftDeterminant_ratio`**:
   $$\mathrm{HeightLe}\Bigl(d \cdot k\bigl(\log b \cdot \mathrm{denominatorBudget} - D.\mathrm{directionAmount}\bigr)\Bigr) \quad\text{on}\quad \frac{Z_j(1)}{Z_j(\theta(\iota)^j)}.$$

### Where this analogy breaks

Describing Stage 06 as "repaying $k$ idle directions by comparing twisted and untwisted Gram determinants through one gluing operator" captures the exact accounting of `LiftVolumeHeight.lean`, but hides two deep structural constraints. First, the untwisted and twisted target spaces live at different congruence levels ($Q = \emptyset$ vs. $Q = \vartheta.Q$), so there is no single operator mapping one target space to the other; `comparisonOp j ϑ` is an entire *family* of operators whose determinants couple only via the twist-independent quadratic form $U^\circ$ on $W_j$. Second, the negative height does not drop out of Stage 02's sharpness theorem directly—it emerges from a four-way cancellation where `AlgebraicPartSize` supplies the downward archimedean credit $-\sum_\tau \mathrm{jetSize}$ and Stage 02 `BoundAttained` caps the upward $p$-adic valuation penalty $+\sum_\tau \mathrm{primeSum}$ strictly below it.
